**Project Title:** Institutional Adoption of the Groundwater Intelligence & Borewell Risk Assessment System (GIBRAS) for Kolar & Chikkaballapur Districts.

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Public Welfare Context

In the semi-arid hard-rock tracts of Kolar and Chikkaballapur, groundwater extraction stages exceed 180–215% ("Over-Exploited"). Smallholder farmers routinely borrow ₹1.5 to ₹3.5 Lakhs at informal interest rates to drill deep borewells (250–450m), experiencing failure rates exceeding 65%. This dynamic is the single largest driver of agrarian debt and distress in eastern Karnataka.

GIBRAS provides a government-grade, scientifically validated decision-support engine designed to:
1. Prevent avoidable drilling expenditure by flagging high-risk geological locations before capital is committed.
2. Provide transparent, bilingual (Kannada & English) advisories to farmers directly via mobile browsers or Gram Panchayat citizen kiosks.
3. Equip government hydrogeologists and Taluk Panchayat officers with standardized digital verification tools.

## 3. Phased Implementation Roadmap

- **Phase I (Months 1–3):** Technical sandbox deployment and calibration against CGWB exploratory drilling logs across 6 pilot taluks (Kolar, Malur, Bangarapet, Chikkaballapur, Gauribidanur, Sidlaghatta).
- **Phase II (Months 4–6):** Integration with Karnataka State Remote Sensing Applications Centre (KSRSAC) lineament GIS server and KSNDMC telemetric rain gauges.
- **Phase III (Months 7–12):** Public rollout via the Karnataka *Grama One* service centers, enabling farmers to print official GIBRAS advisories before applying for farm power connections or bank borewell loans.
