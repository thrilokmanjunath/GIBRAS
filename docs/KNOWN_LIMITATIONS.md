# GIBRAS Known Limitations & Scientific Operational Boundaries

**Mandatory Public Disclosure:** Public Welfare Engineering Standard  
**Target Domain:** Kolar & Chikkaballapur Hard-Rock Aquifers  

---

## 1. Geological Anisotropy & Discrete Fracture Physics
1. **Zero Primary Porosity in Crystalline Basement:**
   Groundwater in granites and gneisses exists strictly within discrete, discontinuous fracture apertures ($\le 1-5\text{ mm}$ width) and shear zones. A location situated 20 meters away from a productive borewell may penetrate completely massive, unfractured rock and yield zero water.
2. **Regional Model vs. Sub-Surface Point Physics:**
   Remote sensing (Sentinel-2), digital elevation models (SRTM 30m), and regional lineament maps provide structural likelihood indicators; **they do not image subterranean water tables directly**. GIBRAS outputs are probabilistic decision-support advisories, **not guarantees**.

---

## 2. Satellite Remote Sensing Physical Boundaries
1. **Zero Sub-Surface Penetration:**
   Optical multispectral imagery (Sentinel-2) observes only the surface skin ($< 1\,\mu\text{m}$). C-band Synthetic Aperture Radar (Sentinel-1) penetrates $\le 5\text{ cm}$ in dry soil. Neither sensor can penetrate 50 to 250 meters of crystalline rock.
2. **Vegetation Anomalies as False Positives:**
   High pre-monsoon NDVI in arid months may indicate intensive groundwater extraction from an already depleted, over-stressed borewell rather than an untapped aquifer.

---

## 3. Depth Recommendation Operational Ceiling
1. **Exponential Drop in Fracture Permeability with Depth:**
   Due to lithostatic overburden pressure ($\approx 2.7\text{ MPa}$ per 100m depth), open fracture frequency declines exponentially beyond 200–220 meters.
2. **The 300+ Meter Drilling Fallacy:**
   Drilling borewells beyond 250 meters in granite-gneiss without verified deep fault structures represents extreme financial risk with a failure rate $>85\%$. GIBRAS enforces an advisory ceiling at 200–220 meters.

---

## 4. Mandatory Ground Verification Prerequisite
Under no circumstances should a farmer or agency initiate drilling without:
1. On-site **Vertical Electrical Sounding (VES)** resistivity sounding conducted by a licensed ground water hydrogeologist.
2. Verification of the mandatory **200-meter statutory spacing** from neighboring functional wells.
3. Checking local cascade tank levels and recent rainfall recharge history.
